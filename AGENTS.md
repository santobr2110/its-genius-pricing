
- Observability pricing has two isolated models (classic UM bands vs levels M1–M4 + NVPS) selected by `modeloObservabilidade`; only one ever produces monitoring cost — keeps saved pricings unchanged.

Tax rates: Treinamento reads `tax_product_codes`/`tax_product_code_rates`; Smart ITO/BodyShop still pick rates from `codigosProdutoImposto.ts` until that switch is approved, because it changes the source of saved rates.
